How the game works

Letters come in phonics order (s, a, t, p, then i, n, m, d…), the order many reading programs use. This lets her start building real words like sat, pin and map early.

Each land has four games: Find the Letter (by name), Big & Little (matching cases), Letter Sounds (first sounds of words), and Rainbow Trace. 6 stars in a land opens the next one. Word Rainbow opens after Cloud Castle and teaches sounding out short words.

A rainbow color is only earned for a first-try right answer. If she guesses wrong, she still has to find the right one, but no color, and that letter comes back later. Wrong taps pause the buttons briefly so tapping everything doesn't work. Tracing has to stay on the letter.

Stars: 3 for a perfect rainbow, 2 for one or two misses, 1 otherwise. A sticker needs 2 or more stars. Stars unlock new looks for her unicorn. After 3 misses in a row, the game drops to 2 choices until she gets one right.
