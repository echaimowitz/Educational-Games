# Educational-Games

## 🦄 Unicorn Letter Land (`unicorn-letters/`)

A rainbow-and-unicorn letter game for preschoolers, made for iPad (works in any modern browser).

**What she learns**
- **Find the Letter**: hear a letter name, tap the right letter
- **Big & Little**: match uppercase to lowercase (A ↔ a)
- **Letter Sounds**: "pizza starts with /p/": the first step toward reading
- **Balloon Pop**: pop every matching letter balloon, without popping a wrong one
- **Memory Match**: flip cards to pair big and little letters
- **Sound Detective**: hear only a sound ("sss") and pick the letter
- **Rainbow Trace**: trace each letter with a finger on handwriting lines
- **Word Rainbow** (opens later): **Build Words** (spell *cat*, *map*, *sun* by sounds) and **Read & Match** (read a word on her own and tap its picture)

Letters come in phonics order (s a t p → i n m d → g o c k → e u r h → b f l j → v w x y z q), so real words open up early. Letters she misses come up more often.

**Prizes**: only *first-try* right answers add a color to the rainbow (guessing doesn't). A missed letter comes back later in the round. Wrong taps pause the buttons briefly, and tracing must stay on the letter. A full rainbow earns 1–3 stars (3 = perfect); 2+ stars also wins a sticker for her sticker book. Stars unlock outfits for her unicorn (manes, coats, horns, wings, crowns…) and open new lands.

**Grown-ups' Corner**: press and hold the ⚙️ on the home screen. It shows letter-by-letter progress and has settings for voice, opening all lands, 4 choices, and resetting progress.

### Playing on an iPad
1. Open `unicorn-letters/index.html` from a web host (e.g., turn on GitHub Pages for this repo, then visit `https://<user>.github.io/Educational-Games/unicorn-letters/`).
2. In Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen app icon.
3. Turn the iPad's silent switch/volume up: the game talks.

**Saving**: progress saves after every rainbow, when the game is closed, and with the 💾 **Save** button on the home screen. Opened from its claude.ai link while signed in, it also saves to your Claude account, so it survives the iPad clearing its memory. Hosted elsewhere, it saves in that browser only.

One self-contained file; it needs internet once to load the fonts.
